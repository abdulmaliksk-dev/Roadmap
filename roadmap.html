Operating rules

These keep the roadmap ambitious without making it impossible.

20 hours normally

Use 25 hours during holidays when you genuinely have the capacity.

DSA stays daily

Two problems per day remains the target. The surrounding workload changes by phase.

Ship every week

A small working artifact beats another unfinished tutorial.

Math stays just-in-time

Learn the mathematics needed to understand AI systems; do not create a separate math degree inside this roadmap.
