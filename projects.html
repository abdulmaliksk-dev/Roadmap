you can do the job.

What makes a project count?

README

Problem, architecture, setup, trade-offs and lessons.

Demo

Something another person can run, open or watch.

Numbers

Latency, retrieval quality, test coverage, cost or another meaningful metric.

Reflection

One limitation and what you would change next.
